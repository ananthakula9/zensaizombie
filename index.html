01 — About

I make Minecraft videos about winning fights

I’m ZensaiZombie (@ZensaiZombie), a Minecraft creator based in India. I started posting to YouTube in June 2026, and my promise is right there in my bio: “I Want to Entertain You By Making Good Content.”

Most of my uploads are long-form Minecraft stories. Some are SMP episodes built around a single goal — hunting down a “god mace”, a “god trident”, or full god armour by mastering spear-mace combat. Others are straight PvP: sword duels against other creators, hunting live PvP streamers, fighting PvP bots, or testing the revamped texture packs other PvP YouTubers use.

I collaborate across the Minecraft creator space — my videos feature YesSmartyPie, SenpaiSpider and VoidWalker, and I’ve recorded on the Himlands SMP.
